De Morgan's law

Turing Comeplete - De Morgan's law

This post is the first of my learning CPU Architecture series by playing Turing Complete. I will capture my notes in this blog :)

The game starts off with Boolean logic, boolean logic is a branch of algebra that deals with variables having only two possible values: true (1) / false (0). Boolean logic is the foundation of digital electronics and programming. The truth tables of the four fundamental gates are symmetrical. You can convert between inverting the input or inverting the output.

Notes

  • You can get from any basic gate to any other basic gate, at most you have to NOT both inputs and output.
  • You can go between OR/NOR by inverting output, the same with AND/NAND. In fact NOR means NOT OR and NAND means NOT AND.
  • Negating the output flips all the bits in the last row of the truth table. Negating the inputs mirrors the last row of the truth table around the center.
